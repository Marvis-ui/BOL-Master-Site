About Beacon of Light (BOL)

Beacon of Light is a Christian fellowship in Vienna, rooted in worship, Scripture and life together. Our story also includes Beacon of Hope Charity, through which our church community shared practical care with others. Both reflect one calling: to carry Christ’s light in faith and service.

Discover Beacon of Hope Charity

Our Vision

To be a welcoming beacon of Christ's love—where every generation feels at home, discovers their God-given purpose, and walks boldly in truth.

Our Mission

To share the Word of God, encourage spiritual growth and serve one another through worship, prayer and fellowship.

Community & Traditions

Beyond Sunday worship, we do life together—celebrating every member's birthday, hosting our annual August Anniversary, Summer Picnic, Chess Championship, and December Brunch.
