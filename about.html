Our Story & Mission

About Beacon of Light (BOL)

Beacon of Light is a Christ-centered church and youth community in Vienna. Dedicated to shining hope, building genuine relationships, and growing together in faith.

Our Vision

To be a welcoming beacon of Christ's love—where every generation feels at home, discovers their God-given purpose, and walks boldly in truth.

Youth & Hour of Power

Our youth ministry is an active, tight-knit family. Through Hour of Power right after Sunday service, we dive into real-life Bible discussions, prayer, and leadership growth.

Community & Traditions

Beyond Sunday worship, we do life together—celebrating every member's birthday, hosting our annual August Anniversary, Summer Picnic, Chess Championship, and December Brunch.

Every Sunday Service

Vibrant worship, heartfelt prayer, and practical teaching from God's Word.

Hour of Power

Youth Sunday School and interactive fellowship immediately following the main service.

1st Sunday Thanksgiving

Our monthly Communion and Thanksgiving celebration honoring God's faithfulness.
