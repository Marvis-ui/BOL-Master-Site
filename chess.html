Every August during our Church Anniversary month, the BOL family battles on the 64 squares in a round-robin points league.

League Format & Rules

No knockout eliminations—everyone plays against every registered player in a round-robin league!

Win = 3 Pts Draw = 1 Pt Loss = 0 Pts
  • Everyone Plays Everyone: Each competitor faces every other player on the roster once.
  • Points Decide the Title: Total accumulated points at the end of all matches crowns the champion.
  • Open to All: Youth and adults of every skill level are welcome to compete.
